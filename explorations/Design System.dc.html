01 · DESIGN INTENT

A counter that looks like a measuring instrument.

Dieter Rams and Teenage Engineering, applied to counting eight 250 ml cups of water. Warm grey paper, near-black ink, hairline rules, a hard grid, and one orange doing exactly two jobs: marking now, and marking the primary act. No gradients, no glass, no water illustrations, no streak confetti, no emoji.

08 · CONTENT

Dry, factual, second person

WRITE
Six cups down. Two to go.
A minimum, not a ceiling. Extra cups are counted.
Weekends run higher. Wednesday is the one to watch.
No nudge within an hour of a cup.
DON'T
Great job! You crushed your goal! 🎉
You're behind — drink up!
I've logged your water for today
Sentence case for prose, uppercase mono for labels and system messages. 24-hour times, litres to two decimals, zero-padded counts (06, not 6). No exclamation marks, no first person, no emoji. Overshooting is acknowledged plainly — “1 cup past goal” — never corrected.

CAVEATS

Fonts load from Google Fonts; no binaries are vendored, so this needs a network connection. Supply the real files for offline use.

This is a design project rather than a design-system project, so the component bundle isn't compiled: the specimens here and in the cards are hand-built, not live mounts of the .jsx files. Moving the files into a design-system project makes them compile.

No brand guidelines, logo, or codebase were supplied — everything here is derived from the prototype in this project.
